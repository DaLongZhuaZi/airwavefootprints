## 0. 四轮实测对比

| 指标 | **NGF 原图（目标）** | v1 | v2 | v4 草稿 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 内容框 | **69% × 56%** | 75% × 78% | 64% × 63% | 69% × 56% |
| 面板均值色 | **#81A8D3** | #588AB9 | #CBDAE8 | #81A8D3 |
| 平均饱和度 | **0.43** | 0.58 | 0.13 | 0.43 |
| 深蓝 (<420) | **42%** | 63% | 0% | 42% |
| 透明背景 | 真实 alpha | 真实 alpha | 真实 alpha | 真实 alpha |

**两轮崩因**：

- v1 太浓 → 我写了 `glossy / saturated`，模型拉满；
- v2 太淡 → 我写了 `high-key / mostly pale / muted steel blue`，模型把深蓝**归零**；
  又把 `thin strokes / etched into frosted glass` 理解成**空心描边字**。
- **教训：颜色只给数值，不给形容词。**

**GPT Image 的两个新坑（本轮）**：

| 现象 | 原因 | 对策 |
|---|---|---|
| 生成了**马赛克棋盘格**背景 | 提示词里的 `transparent background` 被理解成「画出透明的样子」 | 加 `BACKGROUND` 段明令禁止棋盘格；或直接用**纯洋红 #FF00FF** 底色，彻底消除歧义 |
| 波形图形**歪了** | 「圆点 + 3 条同心弧」在训练数据里就是 **WiFi 符号**，模型按记忆里的倾斜角度画 | 把原点从「圆点」换成**脚印**，弧减到 2 条，并写明「用圆规画、数学精确、无旋转倾斜」 |

## 1. 同频窗 AWF · v4（修棋盘格 + 修歪斜）

```
A 1024x1024 app icon foreground on a fully transparent background. Nothing may
touch the canvas edges.

BACKGROUND: the area outside the icon is completely empty, flat and featureless.
Do NOT paint a checkerboard, a grey-and-white grid, a mosaic, a chess pattern or
any texture to represent transparency. Transparency must be a real alpha channel,
not a drawn pattern. If the output cannot carry an alpha channel, fill that area
with perfectly uniform flat magenta #FF00FF instead - one solid colour, no pattern
of any kind.

WINDOW: a glass application window floating in the middle, about 700 x 570 px,
wider than tall (aspect ratio about 1.23:1), centered, with a large empty margin
all around it. Build it from two nested rounded rectangles: an outer frame with a
thin pale-cyan rim (#D9F9FF), then a narrow gap, then an inset screen panel with
its own thin rounded border. Do not fill the canvas with a single rounded plate.

TITLE BAR: the top 13% of the window, slightly lighter than the screen, holding
three small rounded-square buttons at its left. A thin bright line separates the
bar from the screen. At the bar's upper right, a bright specular glint where two
soft semi-transparent white light streaks cross, brightest at the crossing point
and fading out at both ends - a lens flare, not a UI close button, no dark lines.

GLYPH: in the upper-left of the screen, a small upright mark in two parts.
First, a small solid footprint sole seen from directly above: rounded heel, narrow
waist, broad rounded ball, four separated toe dots; filled with a soft vertical
gradient from #FCFDFF to #BCDCFF, thin #386CAE outline, short soft shadow.
Second, two concentric arcs curving around the sole, both centred on exactly the
same point - the centre of the sole. They are perfect circular arcs, upright and
symmetrical, spanning about a quarter circle, opening toward the upper right, evenly
spaced, thin solid strokes about 13 px, the outer one slightly fainter. Draw them
with a compass, mathematically exact: no rotation, no tilt, no skew, no perspective,
no 3D angle, no wobble. The mark must NOT read as a wifi symbol - its origin is a
footprint, not a dot.

LETTERS: the three capitals "AWF", SOLID and FILLED - never hollow, never
outline-only. Occupying x 14%-86% of the window width (about 512 px) and y 56%-83%
of the window height (cap height about 157 px). Wide letter spacing, large open
counters, medium stroke weight (stroke thickness about 20% of the cap height).
Fill: soft vertical gradient from #FCFDFF at the top to #B0D4FF at the bottom.
Thin darker blue outline #386CAE, short soft shadow. Slightly embossed, as if
etched into frosted glass.

COLOR TARGET - the single most important thing. The finished panel averages out to
a MEDIUM periwinkle blue around #81A8D3 with an average saturation around 0.43. It
is clearly blue - never navy-black, never washed-out silver-white or grey. Roughly
40% of the panel surface is deep steel blue (#295796 to #466EA4), about 15% is
medium blue (#6E9BD0), about 25% is pale ice blue (#C1E4FF), and only about 20% is
near-white highlight (#E6F7FF to #FDFFFF). About 90% of the panel pixels should be
blue-dominant (blue channel more than 20 higher than red).

MATERIAL: broad, heavily feathered white-ice sweeps crossing the panel, with the
light-to-dark transition gradual and hazy, like light diffusing through frosted
glass. No hard-edged diagonal highlight bands, no sharp light/dark split. A thin
pale cyan rim light follows every silhouette; raised shapes are softly embossed.

Avoid: checkerboard, chequerboard, grey-and-white grid, mosaic, chess pattern,
transparency pattern, any tiled or textured background, washed-out white, silver or
grey panel, navy-black panel, hard-edged highlight bands, star sparkle flare, wifi
symbol, rotated or tilted or skewed arcs, concentric arcs off-centre, thick uniform
tube strokes, hollow or outline-only letters, hairline letters, thick 3D extrusion,
sticker or emoji look, a solid tile filling the canvas, panel touching the canvas
edges, realistic feet or skin, animal paw prints, antennas, radio towers, clouds,
text other than AWF, watermark.
```

**如果弧线还是歪**：直接删掉两条弧，只留脚印。细线弧对模型太脆弱，
**实心形状才能稳定渲染** —— 脚印本身就是「足迹」，弧只是装饰，不要为了装饰牺牲识别度。

## 2. 波纹足迹（推荐 · 纯图形无窗口）

```
A 1024x1024 app icon graphic on a fully transparent background. The area outside
the mark is completely empty and featureless - do not paint a checkerboard, grid,
mosaic or any texture. Real alpha channel, not a drawn pattern.

A radio-ripple emblem: a small solid footprint sole at the lower left, seen from
directly above (rounded heel, narrow waist, broad ball, four toe dots), with three
concentric arcs curving around it. All arcs share exactly the same centre - the
centre of the sole - and are perfect circular arcs, upright, no rotation, no tilt,
no skew. They are brightest and thickest nearest the sole and fade outward, so the
mark reads as receiving, never emitting. Two smaller, fainter footprints step out
along the outer arcs. Three tiny glowing blips sit between the arcs.

Glossy aqua-glass style: deep steel-blue #295796 base, broad white-ice highlights
#E6F7FF, pale cyan rim light #D9F9FF, embossed pale-ice shapes with soft blue edges.
The finished mark averages to a medium periwinkle blue around #81A8D3, clearly blue,
never washed-out white or grey. Everything inside a centered 700px area. No text.
```

## 3. 脚印入波（最简洁，小尺寸最稳）

```
A 1024x1024 app icon graphic on a fully transparent background. The area outside
the mark is completely empty and featureless - do not paint a checkerboard, grid,
mosaic or any texture. Real alpha channel, not a drawn pattern.

One large clean footprint sole at the center, seen from directly above: rounded
heel, narrow arched waist, broad rounded ball, five separated toe dots with the
biggest toe on the inside. A solid pale ice-white shape with a soft inner bevel,
not an outline. A thin darker groove line across the heel and ball forms a
three-crest signal waveform.

Four concentric ripple arcs spread outward from under the sole, all sharing exactly
the same centre as the sole, evenly spaced, perfect circular arcs with no tilt or
skew; each ripple is thinner and fainter than the one inside it. Two or three tiny
glowing blips float between the outer ripples.

Glossy aqua-glass style: deep steel-blue #295796 base, white-ice highlights
#E6F7FF, pale cyan rim light #D9F9FF. Everything inside a centered 700px area.
No text.
```

## 4. 兜底方案：直接改原图（100% 质感一致）

模型在**颜色**和**细线几何**上都不稳。最可靠的是**保留 NGF 原图，只换两个区域**
（见 [patch-map.png](patch-map.png)）：

| 区域 | 像素坐标 | 面板相对位置 |
|---|---|---|
| `>_` 波形 | (270,353) – (480,476)，210×123 | x 15.7%–45.3%，y 24.7%–46.0% |
| `NGF` 字标 | (257,531) – (769,688)，512×157 | x 13.8%–86.0%，y 55.6%–82.8% |

外框、标题栏、三颗按钮、X 高光、全部玻璃材质**一律不动**。
字标套原 NGF 处理：冰蓝渐变实心 + `#386CAE` 细描边 + 柔和投影，笔画取字高 **20%**。

`docs/logo/foreground-awf-draft.png` 就是按这个方法自动生成的草稿
（真实 alpha、无棋盘格、弧线数学精确、颜色与 NGF 完全一致），可直接当基准图对比。
它的短板是两块被挖掉区域的修补会留一点雾感，正式稿建议在 Figma 里手工重画这两块。

## 5. 三条实操

1. **透明背景**：先加 `BACKGROUND` 段禁止棋盘格；仍失败就改用纯洋红 `#FF00FF` 底再抠图。
2. **一次出 4 张**，只挑构图对的那张。
3. **`AWF` 字标和细弧线别依赖模型**：先生成不带字的图形，后期用矢量补。
